USTC, Hefei

I am a PhD researcher in Computer Science and Technology at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), Hefei. My research primarily focuses on cybersecurity, with a particular interest in IoT security, intrusion detection systems, and the integration of Machine Learning and Deep Learning techniques to strengthen modern security frameworks.

My work addresses emerging challenges in network security, including anomaly detection, threat mitigation, and secure communication protocols, in highly connected environments such as the Internet of Things. I am passionate about developing scalable, adaptive security solutions that ensure data integrity, privacy, and protection against evolving cyberattacks.

Research Areas

Intrusion Detection

AI-based intrusion detection systems for industrial control systems and IoT networks, using big data analytics and ML/DL models.

IoT Security

Threat detection in Internet of Medical Things, lightweight cryptography, and secure communication for constrained devices.

Cyber Security

Protecting critical infrastructure against cyberattacks, including IIoT, SCADA systems, and self-driving vehicles.

Privacy Preservation

Federated learning, zero-trust architectures, and privacy-preserving frameworks for IoT and cloud computing.

Secure Messaging Analysis

Reverse engineering and formal verification of secure messaging protocols using Tamarin Prover and CryptoVerif.

Explainable AI for Security

Explainable learning machines for protecting IIoT-enabled industrial control systems against cyber-attacks.
