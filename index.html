01 — ABOUT ME

Research with a focus on evidence.

I am a PhD researcher in Computer Science and Technology at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), Hefei.

My work examines the security of networked systems: from cryptographic protocols and secure messaging to IoT infrastructures and data-driven intrusion detection. I am interested in connecting implementation-level observations with rigorous security analysis and reproducible experiments.

My research also explores privacy-preserving learning, zero-trust approaches, and explainable machine-learning methods for cyberattack detection.
