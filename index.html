About Me

I'm a PhD researcher at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), working at the intersection of cybersecurity, network security, and IoT security.

My research combines reverse engineering, protocol analysis, and formal verification to uncover security vulnerabilities in real-world secure messaging systems and IoT devices. I enjoy bridging the gap between theoretical cryptography and practical security, identifying weaknesses that matter, and proving properties that hold.

Research Focus

Reverse Engineering

Analyzing closed-source secure messaging protocols to identify weaknesses and verify security claims.

Network Security

Protocol design, traffic analysis, and secure communication over untrusted networks.

IoT Security

Lightweight cryptography, device authentication, and embedded security for resource-constrained devices.

Applied Cryptography

Key exchange protocols, encryption schemes, and secure messaging primitives.

Formal Verification

Proving security properties of protocols using CryptoVerif, ProVerif, and Tamarin.

Vulnerability Research

Identifying and responsibly disclosing security flaws in deployed systems.
